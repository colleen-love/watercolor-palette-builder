Color data: Daniel Smith, Watercolors CIE Lab Coordinates. Lightfastness, staining, granulation, transparency and price series: Daniel Smith's 2021 pigment characteristics sheet, plus retailer listings for King's Royal Blue and McCracken Black. Nine newer paints (the 2025 releases, both Jane's Blacks, Chrome Titanate Yellow) have missing data. Single pigment means one Colour Index pigment on the label; PrimaTek minerals count as single. Reach is the area of the outline around your paints in the a*b* plane. Real mixtures bow inward between distant hues and can bulge outward between neighbors, like yellow and green, so treat it as a guide. Lightness isn't shown.
